Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 June 2026

This covers the GloGraph website (glograph.com) and the email list. It's written to be readable, not lawyerly. GloGraph is an independent project based in Canada.

The short version

  • The app is local-first: your graphs live in .glograph files on your own device. We never see or store them.
  • The website uses cookieless analytics, so there are no tracking cookies and no consent banner.
  • If you join the email list, we store your email to send GloGraph updates. Unsubscribe any time.
  • We don't sell your data or run third-party ad trackers.

The app (GloGraph desktop)

Everything you create stays on your machine in local .glograph files (SQLite). The app works fully offline and does not send your graphs anywhere. Its optional built-in HTTP API binds only to your own computer (127.0.0.1) and is off by default.

Website analytics

We use Plausible Analytics to count visits and which buttons get clicked, so we can tell whether people find GloGraph useful. Plausible is cookieless, does not collect personal data, and does not build cross-site profiles. We only ever see aggregate numbers.

Email list

If you submit your email, it is stored with Buttondown (our email provider) so we can send you GloGraph news and launch updates. We won't use it for anything else. Every email includes an unsubscribe link, and you can ask us to delete you at any time (see Contact).

What we don't do

No selling or renting your data. No third-party advertising trackers. No cookie walls.

Your choices & rights

You can unsubscribe from any email, or contact us to access or delete your data. We honor the rights you have under your local laws, including PIPEDA (Canada), the GDPR (EU/UK), and the CCPA (California).

Contact

Questions or data requests: glograph.app@gmail.com.

This policy may be updated as the product grows; the "last updated" date above reflects the current version. GloGraph is free to download and use.
